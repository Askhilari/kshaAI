The Problem

For too long, the carbon market has been inaccessible to small and medium-sized farms. The process is bogged down by multiple challenges.

Complex Paperwork

Lengthy and technical Project Design Documents (PDDs) that are difficult to complete without specialized knowledge.

Opaque Processes

Navigating the regulations of registries like Verra or India's own CCTS can be confusing without expert help.

Lengthy Timelines

Traditional registration and validation can take 12 to 18 months, delaying potential income for farmers.

High Costs

Professional consultancy fees and validation costs make it financially unviable for smaller projects.

Our AI-Powered Solution

We cut through the complexity. Our platform uses specialized AI to automate and guide you through the entire carbon credit registration process with the Indian Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS).

AI for Paperwork & Documentation

  • Automated PDD Generator: Simple questionnaire about your farm practices automatically drafts your Project Design Document
